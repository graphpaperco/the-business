# The Business - Company Repository

A comprehensive, organized repository for managing all aspects of your company operations.

## 📖 Overview

This repository serves as the central hub for company documentation, processes, workflows, and institutional knowledge. It's designed to scale with your company from early-stage startup through growth and beyond.

## 🎯 Purpose

- **Centralized Documentation** - Single source of truth for all company information
- **Standardized Processes** - Consistent workflows across all functions
- **Efficient Operations** - Streamlined processes with clear guidelines
- **AI-Assisted Work** - Optimized for working with Claude AI
- **Scalable Structure** - Grows with your company needs
- **Institutional Knowledge** - Preserve and share company knowledge

## 🔄 Common Workflows

### Cross-Functional Processes

**New Employee Hire**
1. HR: Recruiting and offer letter
2. Finance: Budget approval and payroll setup
3. Legal: Employment agreement review
4. Admin: Equipment and workspace setup

**Fundraising Round**
1. Fundraising: Pitch deck, investor pipeline
2. Finance: Financial projections and models
3. Legal: Financing documents and due diligence
4. Investor Relations: New investor onboarding

**Board Meeting**
1. Investor Relations: Meeting coordination and materials
2. Finance: Financial reports and metrics
3. HR: Headcount and team updates
4. All Teams: Functional updates

**Vendor Contract**
1. Admin: Vendor sourcing and evaluation
2. Legal: Contract review and negotiation
3. Finance: Budget approval and payment setup

## 📅 Operating Cadence

### Monthly
- **Finance:** Monthly close, financial reports
- **Investor Relations:** Monthly investor update
- **All Functions:** Metrics review, team meetings

### Quarterly
- **Board Meeting:** Prepare materials, present to board
- **Business Review:** Quarterly performance review
- **Finance:** Budget vs. actuals analysis
- **All Functions:** OKR review and planning

### Annually
- **Finance:** Annual budget and planning
- **HR:** Performance reviews, compensation planning
- **Legal:** Corporate compliance, governance
- **All Functions:** Strategic planning, goal setting

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Company-Wide Metrics

**Financial Health**
- Revenue (MRR/ARR)
- Gross margin percentage
- Monthly burn rate
- Cash runway (months)
- Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
- Lifetime value (LTV)

**Growth Metrics**
- Revenue growth rate
- Customer acquisition (new logos)
- User/engagement growth
- Market expansion

**Efficiency Metrics**
- LTV/CAC ratio
- Gross margin
- Operating expenses as % of revenue
- Rule of 40 (Growth + Profitability)

**Team Metrics**
- Headcount and growth rate
- Employee retention rate
- Time to hire
- Employee satisfaction scores

This repository contains sensitive company information:

- **Legal documents** - Contracts, corporate records
