# Chart of Accounts - Graph Paper LLC

Simplified chart of accounts for single-member LLC operations.

## Income

### Consulting Revenue
**Account:** 4000 - Consulting Income
**Description:** Revenue from consulting services
**Tax Category:** Business Income

### Product Revenue
**Account:** 4100 - Product Sales
**Description:** Revenue from Heirloom OS or other products
**Tax Category:** Business Income

### DBA Revenue - Ladybug Gifts
**Account:** 4200 - Ladybug Gifts Sales
**Description:** Revenue from pop-up shop and gift sales
**Tax Category:** Business Income

### DBA Revenue - Bless This Mess
**Account:** 4300 - Bless This Mess Services
**Description:** Revenue from organizing/decluttering services
**Tax Category:** Business Income

### Other Income
**Account:** 4900 - Other Income
**Description:** Miscellaneous business income, interest, etc.
**Tax Category:** Other Income

## Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) - If Applicable

### Inventory Purchases

## Operating Expenses

### Home Office
**Account:** 6000 - Home Office Expense
**Description:** Home office deduction (simplified or actual)
**Tax Category:** Deductible Expense
**Note:** Track square footage and percentage of home used

### Technology & Software
**Account:** 6100 - Software & Subscriptions
**Description:** SaaS tools, hosting, domains, cloud services
**Tax Category:** Deductible Expense
**Examples:** GitHub, AWS, Google Workspace, Adobe Creative Cloud

### Equipment & Supplies
**Account:** 6200 - Equipment & Supplies
**Description:** Computer equipment, office supplies, furniture
**Tax Category:** Deductible Expense (or depreciate if >$2,500)

### Professional Development
**Account:** 6300 - Education & Training
**Description:** Courses, books, conferences, certifications
**Tax Category:** Deductible Expense

### Marketing & Advertising
**Account:** 6400 - Marketing & Advertising
**Description:** Website, ads, promotional materials
**Tax Category:** Deductible Expense

### Professional Services
**Account:** 6500 - Professional Fees
**Description:** CPA, attorney, consultants
**Tax Category:** Deductible Expense

### Insurance
**Account:** 6600 - Insurance
**Description:** Professional liability, business insurance
**Tax Category:** Deductible Expense

### Bank & Payment Processing Fees
**Account:** 6700 - Bank & Merchant Fees
**Description:** Bank fees, Stripe/PayPal fees, transaction costs
**Tax Category:** Deductible Expense

### Business Licenses & Fees
**Account:** 6800 - Licenses & Permits
**Description:** Business licenses, filing fees, permits
**Tax Category:** Deductible Expense

### Vehicle Expenses
**Account:** 6900 - Vehicle Expenses
**Description:** Mileage or actual vehicle expenses
**Tax Category:** Deductible Expense
**Note:** Keep mileage log; can use standard mileage rate

### Meals (Business)
**Account:** 7000 - Meals & Entertainment
**Description:** Business meals (50% deductible)
**Tax Category:** Deductible Expense (50%)
**Note:** Document business purpose

### Travel
**Account:** 7100 - Travel
**Description:** Business travel expenses
**Tax Category:** Deductible Expense

### Telephone & Internet
**Account:** 7200 - Phone & Internet
**Description:** Business portion of phone/internet
**Tax Category:** Deductible Expense
**Note:** Only business use percentage is deductible

### Taxes & Licenses
**Account:** 7300 - Taxes & Licenses
**Description:** Sales tax collected/paid, business property tax
**Tax Category:** Deductible Expense

### Miscellaneous
**Account:** 7900 - Miscellaneous Expense
**Description:** Other business expenses not categorized above
**Tax Category:** Deductible Expense

## Other Accounts

## Setup Instructions

1. **Use this chart** to categorize all income and expenses
2. **Track consistently** - Assign every transaction to an account
3. **Review monthly** - Run P&L report to see income/expenses by category
4. **Simplify** - Start with these categories, add more only if needed
5. **Tax time** - These categories map directly to Schedule C lines
