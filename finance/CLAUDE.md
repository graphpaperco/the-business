# Finance Function - Claude Workflows

This directory contains all finance-related documentation, processes, and workflows.

## Primary Responsibilities

- Financial planning and analysis (FP&A)
- Budgeting and forecasting
- Accounting and bookkeeping
- Financial reporting
- Cash flow management
- Expense management
- Payroll processing
- Tax compliance and planning
- Financial controls and audits

## Key Workflows

### Monthly Financial Close
1. Reconcile all accounts (bank, credit cards, loans)
2. Review and categorize all transactions
3. Generate P&L, balance sheet, and cash flow statements
4. Compare actuals vs. budget
5. Document any variances and prepare explanations
6. Archive monthly financials with date stamp

### Budget Management
1. Collect budget requests from all departments
2. Consolidate into master budget model
3. Review with leadership team
4. Track actual spend vs. budget monthly
5. Update forecasts based on actuals
6. Flag any budget concerns early

### Expense Processing
1. Collect expense reports from team members
2. Verify receipts and business purpose
3. Categorize expenses properly
4. Process reimbursements
5. Update accounting system
6. Track spending by category and department

### Cash Flow Forecasting
1. Project cash inflows (revenue, funding, etc.)
2. Project cash outflows (payroll, expenses, etc.)
3. Identify any cash gaps
4. Plan for financing needs
5. Update forecast weekly or bi-weekly
6. Communicate cash position to leadership

### Financial Reporting
1. Generate monthly management reports
2. Create board-level financial packages
3. Prepare annual financial statements
4. Document key metrics and KPIs
5. Provide variance analysis
6. Include forward-looking projections

## Claude Assistant Instructions

When working in the finance directory:

1. **Maintain confidentiality** - Financial data is sensitive
2. **Follow GAAP principles** - Use standard accounting practices
3. **Document thoroughly** - Keep clear audit trails
4. **Use templates** - Standardize financial documents
5. **Version control** - Track changes to financial models
6. **Review carefully** - Double-check all calculations
7. **Communicate clearly** - Explain financial concepts simply

## Key Metrics to Track

- Revenue (MRR/ARR for SaaS)
- Gross margin
- Operating expenses by category
- Burn rate
- Runway (months of cash)
- Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
- Lifetime value (LTV)
- Rule of 40 (Growth + Profitability)

## Best Practices

1. **Monthly cadence** - Close books within 5 business days
2. **Real-time visibility** - Keep accounting system current
3. **Variance analysis** - Understand and explain differences
4. **Forward-looking** - Always include projections
5. **Scenario planning** - Model best/base/worst cases
6. **Cash focus** - Cash is king, monitor closely
7. **Documentation** - Keep clear records for audits
