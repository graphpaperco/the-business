# Tax Deadlines - Graph Paper LLC

## Single-Member LLC Tax Overview

As a single-member LLC, Graph Paper LLC is a **disregarded entity** for federal tax purposes. This means:
- Business income/expenses reported on your personal tax return (Form 1040, Schedule C)
- No separate business tax return required
- You pay self-employment tax on net business income
- Must make quarterly estimated tax payments if you expect to owe $1,000+ in taxes

---

## Federal Tax Deadlines

### Quarterly Estimated Taxes (Form 1040-ES)

**Q1 (Jan 1 - Mar 31)**
- **Due:** April 15
- **Income Period:** January 1 - March 31
- **Payment Method:** EFTPS.gov or IRS Direct Pay

**Q2 (Apr 1 - May 31)**
- **Due:** June 15
- **Income Period:** April 1 - May 31

**Q3 (Jun 1 - Aug 31)**
- **Due:** September 15
- **Income Period:** June 1 - August 31

**Q4 (Sep 1 - Dec 31)**
- **Due:** January 15 (following year)
- **Income Period:** September 1 - December 31

**Calculating Estimated Taxes:**
- Pay 100% of prior year's tax liability (110% if AGI > $150K), OR
- Pay 90% of current year's expected tax liability
- Use IRS Form 1040-ES worksheet or tax software

### Annual Tax Return

**Form 1040 + Schedule C**
- **Due:** April 15 (following year)
- **Extensions:** File Form 4868 for automatic 6-month extension (to Oct 15)
- **Note:** Extension to file ≠ extension to pay; taxes still due April 15

**Schedule SE (Self-Employment Tax)**
- Filed with Form 1040
- Calculates Social Security and Medicare tax (15.3% of net income)

**Includes:**
- Schedule C: Profit or Loss from Business
- Schedule SE: Self-Employment Tax
- Form 8829: Home Office Deduction (if using actual expense method)

## Arkansas State Tax Deadlines

### Individual Income Tax
**AR1000 (Arkansas Individual Income Tax)**
- **Due:** April 15 (same as federal)
- **Extensions:** Automatic if federal extension filed
- **Business Income:** Flows from federal Schedule C

### Sales Tax (if applicable)

**Monthly Filers:**
- **Due:** 20th of following month
- **File:** Arkansas Taxpayer Access Point (ATAP)

**Quarterly Filers:**
- **Q1 (Jan-Mar):** Due April 20
- **Q2 (Apr-Jun):** Due July 20
- **Q3 (Jul-Sep):** Due October 20
- **Q4 (Oct-Dec):** Due January 20

**Annual Filers:**
- **Due:** January 20 (following year)

**Filing Schedule:** Determined by sales volume; check with AR DFA

## Other Important Deadlines

### Form 1099-NEC (if you pay contractors)
- **Issue to Contractor:** January 31
- **File with IRS:** January 31 (electronically) or February 28 (paper)
- **Required if:** You paid contractor $600+ during the year

### Arkansas Annual Franchise Tax
- **Due:** May 1
- **Amount:** $150
- **File:** Arkansas Secretary of State website
- **Required:** Even if no business activity

### W-9 Collection
- **When:** Before paying any contractor/vendor
- **Why:** Need their SSN/EIN for 1099 reporting
- **Best Practice:** Get W-9 before first payment

## Tax Payment Methods

### Federal
- **EFTPS** (Electronic Federal Tax Payment System): https://www.eftps.gov
  - Enrollment required (takes 5-7 business days)
  - Schedule payments in advance
- **IRS Direct Pay:** https://www.irs.gov/payments/direct-pay
  - No enrollment required
  - Pay directly from bank account

### Arkansas
- **ATAP** (Arkansas Taxpayer Access Point): https://atap.arkansas.gov
  - For sales tax and other state taxes
  - ACH payment or credit card

## Tax Planning Checklist

### Quarterly (Before Each Estimated Payment)
- [ ] Calculate income received this quarter
- [ ] Subtract business expenses
- [ ] Estimate self-employment tax (15.3% of net income)
- [ ] Calculate estimated income tax
- [ ] Make estimated tax payment
- [ ] Document calculation and payment confirmation

### Monthly
- [ ] Review profit & loss statement
- [ ] Ensure all expenses are categorized properly
- [ ] Set aside ~30% of net income for taxes
- [ ] Reconcile accounts

### Year-End (December)
- [ ] Review total income and expenses
- [ ] Maximize deductible expenses if needed (equipment, supplies)
- [ ] Calculate final estimated payment (due Jan 15)
- [ ] Prepare records for CPA
- [ ] Generate annual reports from accounting software

### Tax Season (January - April)
- [ ] Issue 1099s to contractors (by Jan 31)
- [ ] Gather all income documentation
- [ ] Compile deductible expense receipts
- [ ] Calculate home office deduction
- [ ] Mileage log summary
- [ ] Meet with CPA or prepare return
- [ ] File by April 15 (or extension)

## Records to Keep

**Income:**
- Client invoices and payment records
- 1099s received from clients
- Sales receipts (Ladybug Gifts, Bless This Mess)
- Bank statements showing deposits

**Expenses:**
- Receipts for all deductible expenses
- Credit card statements
- Mileage log (if claiming vehicle expenses)
- Home office calculation (square footage, utilities)
- Proof of business meals with notes on business purpose

**Retention:** Keep tax records for at least 7 years

## Tax Savings Strategies

### Deductible Expenses
- Home office (simplified or actual method)
- Health insurance premiums (self-employed)
- Retirement contributions (SEP IRA, Solo 401k)
- Qualified business income deduction (QBI - up to 20% of net income)
- Business equipment (Section 179 deduction or bonus depreciation)

### Retirement Contributions
**SEP IRA:**
- Contribute up to 25% of net self-employment income
- Maximum $66,000 (2023)
- Tax-deductible

**Solo 401(k):**
- Employee contribution: up to $22,500 (2023) + $7,500 catch-up if 50+
- Employer contribution: up to 25% of net income
- Combined max: $66,000 (2023)
- Tax-deductible
