## Feature Prioritization Framework

**How we prioritize:**

### Impact vs. Effort Matrix
```
High Impact, Low Effort  → Do First (Quick Wins)
High Impact, High Effort → Do Second (Major Projects)
Low Impact, Low Effort   → Do Third (Fill-ins)
Low Impact, High Effort  → Don't Do (Money Pit)
```

### Scoring Criteria
Each feature scored 1-10 on:
- **User Value:** How valuable to users?
- **Business Value:** Impact on business goals?
- **Effort:** Development complexity (reverse score)
- **Strategic Fit:** Alignment with vision?

**Priority Score = (User Value + Business Value + Strategic Fit) / Effort**
