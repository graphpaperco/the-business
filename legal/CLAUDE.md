# Legal Function - Claude Workflows

This directory contains all legal-related documentation, contracts, and compliance workflows.

## Primary Responsibilities

- Corporate governance and compliance
- Contract drafting, review, and negotiation
- Intellectual property protection
- Employment law compliance
- Fundraising legal support
- Regulatory compliance
- Risk management
- Litigation management
- Privacy and data protection
- Vendor and partnership agreements

## Key Workflows

### Contract Review and Execution
1. Receive contract request
2. Assign to appropriate reviewer
3. Review terms and identify issues
4. Negotiate changes with counterparty
5. Obtain internal approvals
6. Execute via DocuSign or wet signature
7. File signed copy in contract repository
8. Set calendar reminders for key dates
9. Track obligations and renewals

### Entity Formation and Maintenance
1. Choose entity type (C-Corp, LLC, etc.)
2. File incorporation documents
3. Adopt bylaws and corporate governance docs
4. Issue founder stock
5. Obtain EIN from IRS
6. Open bank accounts
7. File annual reports and franchise taxes
8. Maintain corporate records
9. Hold required board meetings

### Intellectual Property Protection
1. Identify IP assets (patents, trademarks, copyrights)
2. Ensure all IP is assigned to company
3. File trademark applications
4. Consider patent applications for inventions
5. Protect trade secrets
6. Include IP provisions in employee agreements
7. Monitor for infringement
8. License IP appropriately

### Fundraising Legal Support
1. Prepare or update corporate documents
2. Draft or review term sheet
3. Conduct legal due diligence
4. Draft financing documents (stock purchase agreement, etc.)
5. Coordinate investor signatures
6. File required notices (Form D, etc.)
7. Update cap table and certificates
8. File amended charter if needed

### Employment Agreement Management
1. Use standard employment agreement templates
2. Include IP assignment and confidentiality
3. Define compensation and benefits
4. Specify at-will employment (if applicable)
5. Include non-compete/non-solicit (if enforceable)
6. Review with legal counsel
7. Execute before start date
8. File in employee personnel file

## Contract Management

### Key Contract Terms to Review

**Commercial Terms**
- Scope of services/deliverables
- Payment terms and pricing
- Term and renewal
- Termination rights
- Performance metrics/SLAs

**Legal Terms**
- Liability caps and exclusions
- Indemnification obligations
- Warranty disclaimers
- IP ownership and licensing
- Confidentiality
- Data protection
- Dispute resolution

**Risk Areas**
- Unlimited liability
- One-sided indemnities
- Broad warranties
- Unfavorable IP terms
- Long lock-in periods
- Auto-renewals
- Change of control provisions

### Approval Authority Matrix

| Contract Value | Approver |
|---------------|----------|
| < $10K | Department Head |
| $10K - $50K | VP/Director + Legal |
| $50K - $100K | Executive Team + Legal |
| > $100K | CEO + CFO + Legal |

### Contract Lifecycle

1. **Request** - Business team submits contract request
2. **Draft** - Legal drafts or receives counterparty draft
3. **Review** - Legal and business review terms
4. **Negotiate** - Back and forth with counterparty
5. **Approve** - Obtain required internal approvals
6. **Execute** - Sign via DocuSign or wet signature
7. **Store** - File in contract repository
8. **Track** - Monitor obligations, renewals, expirations
9. **Renew/Terminate** - Action at end of term

## Intellectual Property

### Types of IP

**Patents**
- Utility patents (inventions, processes)
- Design patents (ornamental designs)
- Consider provisional applications
- Work with patent attorney

**Trademarks**
- Company name
- Product names
- Logos
- Tag lines
- File federal applications with USPTO

**Copyrights**
- Software code
- Marketing materials
- Content and documentation
- Automatic but can register

**Trade Secrets**
- Confidential information
- Proprietary processes
- Customer lists
- Protect via NDAs and security

### IP Best Practices

1. **Assignment** - All IP assigned to company
2. **Employees** - IP assignment in employment agreements
3. **Contractors** - Work-for-hire provisions
4. **Open Source** - Track and comply with licenses
5. **Protection** - File trademarks, consider patents
6. **Documentation** - Maintain invention disclosures
7. **Confidentiality** - Protect trade secrets
8. **Licensing** - Clear terms for IP licenses

## Privacy and Data Protection

### Requirements
- Privacy policy (required in most jurisdictions)
- Terms of service
- Cookie policy (if applicable)
- Data processing agreements (for vendors)
- GDPR compliance (if EU users)
- CCPA compliance (if California users)
- Data security measures
- Breach notification procedures

### Best Practices
- Privacy by design
- Minimize data collection
- Obtain proper consents
- Honor user rights (access, deletion)
- Vet vendors for data protection
- Encrypt sensitive data
- Regular security audits
- Incident response plan

## Working with External Counsel

### When to Involve Counsel
- Fundraising transactions
- Significant contracts (> $100K)
- Litigation or disputes
- Regulatory investigations
- Complex IP matters
- M&A transactions
- Novel legal issues
- High-risk situations

### Managing Counsel Relationships
- Set clear scope and budget
- Request flat fees when possible
- Review invoices carefully
- Maintain organized communication
- Leverage counsel for strategy, not admin
- Build long-term relationships
- Consider secondment for busy periods

## Risk Management

### Common Startup Legal Risks
- Founder disputes
- IP ownership issues
- Employment law violations
- Contract breaches
- Regulatory non-compliance
- Privacy breaches
- Product liability
- Securities law violations

### Mitigation Strategies
- Proper founder agreements
- Clear IP assignments
- Strong employment practices
- Careful contract review
- Compliance monitoring
- Security measures
- Insurance coverage
- Regular legal audits

## Insurance Coverage

### Recommended Policies
- D&O (Directors & Officers) insurance
- E&O (Errors & Omissions) / Professional liability
- Cyber liability insurance
- General liability insurance
- Workers' compensation (required)
- Property insurance
- Employment practices liability (EPLI)

## Red Flags to Avoid

- Missing IP assignments
- Unclear founder equity
- Poor corporate records
- Unsigned contracts
- Non-compliance with regulations
- Inadequate privacy protections
- No insurance coverage
- Ignoring legal deadlines
- Verbal agreements
- One-sided unfavorable terms
