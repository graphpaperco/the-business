## 2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

**"Confidential Information"** means any information disclosed by one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party"), whether orally, in writing, or by inspection of tangible objects, including without limitation:

- Technical information: algorithms, code, designs, specifications, data, software
- Business information: financial data, customer lists, business plans, marketing strategies
- Any information marked as "Confidential," "Proprietary," or with a similar designation
- Any information that reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information and circumstances of disclosure

**Confidential Information shall not include information that:**

1. Was publicly known and made generally available prior to disclosure by Disclosing Party
2. Becomes publicly known and made generally available after disclosure by Disclosing Party through no action or inaction of Receiving Party
3. Is already in the possession of Receiving Party at the time of disclosure without an obligation of confidentiality
4. Is obtained by Receiving Party from a third party without a breach of such third party's obligations of confidentiality
5. Is independently developed by Receiving Party without use of or reference to Disclosing Party's Confidential Information

## 3. OBLIGATIONS OF RECEIVING PARTY

### 3.1 Non-Use and Non-Disclosure
Receiving Party agrees:

(a) To hold and maintain Confidential Information in strict confidence

(b) To use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose

(c) Not to disclose Confidential Information to third parties without prior written consent of Disclosing Party

(d) To protect Confidential Information using the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information, but in no event less than reasonable care

### 3.2 Permitted Disclosures
Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information only to its employees, contractors, advisors, or representatives who:

(a) Have a legitimate need to know for the Purpose

(b) Have been informed of the confidential nature of the information

(c) Are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as restrictive as those in this Agreement

Receiving Party remains responsible for any breach by such persons.

### 3.3 Required Disclosures
If Receiving Party is required by law, court order, or government regulation to disclose Confidential Information, Receiving Party shall:

(a) Promptly notify Disclosing Party of such requirement (unless prohibited by law)

(b) Cooperate with Disclosing Party's efforts to seek protective order or other confidential treatment

(c) Disclose only that portion of Confidential Information legally required

## 4. OWNERSHIP AND RETURN OF MATERIALS

### 4.1 Ownership
All Confidential Information remains the sole property of Disclosing Party. No license or rights in Confidential Information or any intellectual property rights are granted except as expressly set forth herein.

### 4.2 Return or Destruction
Upon request by Disclosing Party or termination of this Agreement, Receiving Party shall promptly:

(a) Return all Confidential Information and copies thereof, OR

(b) Destroy all Confidential Information and certify such destruction in writing

(c) Exception: May retain one archival copy solely for legal compliance purposes, subject to continued confidentiality obligations

## When to Use an NDA

**Use an NDA when:**
- Discussing proprietary business ideas
- Sharing technical specifications or code
- Disclosing customer lists or business strategies
- Providing financial information
- Sharing trade secrets or confidential processes

**You may not need an NDA for:**
- General business discussions
- Public information
- Standard service descriptions
- Already published material

---

## Types of NDAs

**Unilateral (One-Way):**
- Only one party discloses information
- Other party receives and protects
- Use when: You're sharing info but not receiving any

**Mutual (Two-Way):**
- Both parties disclose and receive information
- Both parties bound by same obligations
- Use when: Both sides sharing confidential info (most common)
