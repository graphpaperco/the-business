# Legal

This directory contains all legal documentation, contracts, and compliance materials.

## Purpose

The legal function is responsible for managing all legal matters for the company, including:
- Corporate governance and compliance
- Contract drafting and review
- Intellectual property protection
- Employment law compliance
- Regulatory compliance
- Risk management

## Key Documents

- **CLAUDE.md** - Detailed workflows and processes for legal operations
- **corporate/** - Formation documents, board materials, stock records
- **contracts/** - Customer, vendor, and partner agreements
- **employment/** - Employment agreement templates and executed docs
- **intellectual-property/** - Trademarks, patents, IP assignments
- **fundraising/** - Financing documents and legal materials
- **privacy/** - Privacy policy, terms of service, GDPR compliance
