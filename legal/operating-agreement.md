### 1. Company Information
- **LLC Name:** Graph Paper LLC
- **State of Formation:** Arkansas
- **Formation Date:** [Date]
- **Principal Office:** [Address]
- **Registered Agent:** [Name and address]

### 2. Member Information
- **Sole Member:** [Your Name]
- **Address:** [Your Address]
- **Membership Interest:** 100%
- **Initial Capital Contribution:** $[Amount]

### 3. Management
- **Management Structure:** Member-managed
- **Managing Member:** [Your Name]
- **Authority:** Managing Member has full authority to bind the LLC and make all business decisions

### 4. Capital Contributions
- **Initial Contribution:** $[Amount] on [Date]
- **Additional Contributions:** Not required unless determined by Member
- **Capital Account:** Maintained for Member showing contributions and distributions

### 5. Distributions
- Distributions at discretion of Member
- No required distribution schedule
- Distributions reduce Member's capital account

### 6. Books and Records
- **Fiscal Year:** Calendar Year (January 1 - December 31)
- **Accounting Method:** Cash basis [or Accrual]
- **Records Location:** [Physical or digital location]
- **Access:** Member has full access at all times

### 7. Banking
- **Bank Account:** Business checking account at [Bank Name]
- **Signatories:** Member has sole signing authority
- **Requirements:** All company funds kept separate from personal funds

### 8. Taxes
- **Tax Treatment:** Disregarded entity (reported on Member's personal return)
- **Tax Returns:** Member files Schedule C with Form 1040
- **Tax ID:** [EIN]

### 9. Dissolution
- LLC may be dissolved by Member at any time
- Upon dissolution:
  - Pay all debts and obligations
  - Distribute remaining assets to Member
  - File certificate of dissolution with state

### 10. Amendments
- This agreement may be amended at any time by Member in writing

### 11. Indemnification
- LLC shall indemnify Member from liabilities arising from good-faith business activities

### 12. Limitation of Liability
- Member's liability limited to capital contribution
- Member not personally liable for LLC debts except where required by law

## Important Provisions for Arkansas LLCs

### Annual Franchise Tax
- Due May 1 each year
- File with Arkansas Secretary of State
- Fee: $150

### Annual Report
- File with Secretary of State
- Update any changes to business information

### Registered Agent
- Must maintain registered agent in Arkansas
- Agent must have physical Arkansas address
- Update within 30 days of any change
