# Administrative (Admin)

This directory contains all administrative processes, vendor management, and operational support.

## Purpose

The administrative function is responsible for managing day-to-day operations and supporting all teams, including:
- Office management and facilities
- Vendor and supplier management
- Procurement and purchasing
- IT and systems administration
- Travel coordination
- Equipment and asset management

## Key Documents

- **CLAUDE.md** - Detailed workflows and processes for administrative operations
- **vendors/** - Vendor list, contracts, and evaluations
- **procurement/** - Purchase requests, orders, and policies
- **office/** - Lease agreements, floor plans, office policies
- **it-systems/** - Software licenses, hardware inventory, IT documentation
- **travel/** - Travel policy and itineraries
- **assets/** - Asset register and equipment tracking
