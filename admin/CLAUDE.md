# Administrative Function - Claude Workflows

This directory contains all administrative processes, operations, and support workflows.

## Primary Responsibilities

- Office management and facilities
- Vendor and supplier management
- Procurement and purchasing
- IT and systems administration
- Travel and event coordination
- Administrative support services
- Equipment and asset management
- Mail and shipping services
- Office supplies and inventory
- Reception and visitor management

## Key Workflows

### Vendor Onboarding and Management
1. Identify vendor need and requirements
2. Research and evaluate potential vendors
3. Request proposals and quotes
4. Conduct vendor selection
5. Negotiate terms and pricing
6. Execute contract (coordinate with legal)
7. Set up in accounting system
8. Onboard vendor and communicate process
9. Monitor performance and SLAs
10. Conduct periodic vendor reviews

### Purchase Request Process
1. Employee submits purchase request
2. Verify budget availability
3. Obtain required approvals
4. Source vendor (existing or new)
5. Place order
6. Track delivery
7. Receive and inspect items
8. Process invoice for payment
9. Update asset register if applicable

### New Employee Setup
1. Receive new hire information from HR
2. Order computer and equipment
3. Set up email and system accounts
4. Prepare workspace
5. Configure phone and collaboration tools
6. Install required software
7. Schedule IT orientation
8. Provide access badge/keys
9. Test all systems before start date

### Office Space Management
1. Manage lease agreements
2. Coordinate with building management
3. Plan office layout and seating
4. Manage office expansions or moves
5. Ensure health and safety compliance
6. Coordinate cleaning services
7. Maintain office security
8. Manage visitor access

### Travel Coordination
1. Book flights and accommodations
2. Arrange ground transportation
3. Prepare travel itinerary
4. Provide travel policy guidelines
5. Support travel insurance if needed
6. Coordinate group travel for events
7. Track travel expenses
8. Maintain preferred vendor relationships

## Procurement Policy

### Approval Limits
| Amount | Approver |
|--------|----------|
| < $500 | Department Head |
| $500 - $5K | VP/Director |
| $5K - $25K | Executive Team |
| > $25K | CEO + CFO |

### Preferred Vendor Program
- Negotiate volume discounts
- Establish standard terms
- Streamline ordering process
- Improve payment terms
- Build strategic relationships

### Purchase Guidelines
1. Always get 3 quotes for purchases > $10K
2. Use preferred vendors when available
3. Follow approval matrix strictly
4. Document business justification
5. Consider total cost of ownership
6. Evaluate vendor stability
7. Review contracts carefully

## Travel Policy

### Booking Guidelines
- Book flights 2+ weeks in advance
- Use preferred airlines and hotels
- Economy class for domestic flights < 5 hours
- Business class for international long-haul
- Reasonable accommodations (not luxury)
- Use company travel tools/partners

### Expense Reimbursement
- Submit within 30 days
- Provide itemized receipts
- Categorize expenses properly
- Follow approval process
- Use expense management software

## Best Practices

1. **Documentation** - Document all processes and procedures
2. **Automation** - Automate repetitive tasks where possible
3. **Standardization** - Create templates and standard processes
4. **Communication** - Keep team informed of changes
5. **Proactive** - Anticipate needs before they become urgent
6. **Customer service** - Treat internal teams as customers
7. **Continuous improvement** - Regularly review and optimize
8. **Risk management** - Identify and mitigate operational risks

## Red Flags to Avoid

- Maverick spending (bypassing approvals)
- Poor vendor management
- Inadequate documentation
- Security vulnerabilities
- Single points of failure
- Outdated systems
- Lack of backup/redundancy
- Untracked assets
- Missing contracts
- Reactive instead of proactive
