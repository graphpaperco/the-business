# Compliance Calendar - Graph Paper LLC

A single view of all important deadlines and recurring obligations.

## Annual Deadlines

### January
- **Jan 15:** Q4 Estimated Tax Payment (IRS)
- **Jan 31:** Issue 1099s to contractors (if paid $600+)

### February
- **Feb 28/Mar 31:** File 1099s with IRS

### March
- No major deadlines (prepare for April tax filing)

### April
- **Apr 15:** Q1 Estimated Tax Payment (IRS)
- **Apr 15:** File personal tax return (includes Schedule C for LLC)
- **Apr 15:** File Arkansas individual income tax return

### May
- **May 1:** Arkansas Annual Franchise Tax Report due

### June
- **Jun 15:** Q2 Estimated Tax Payment (IRS)

### July-August
- No major deadlines

### September
- **Sep 15:** Q3 Estimated Tax Payment (IRS)

### October-November
- No major deadlines

### December
- **Dec 31:** Last day for business expenses to count for current tax year
- **Dec 31:** Review year-end financials

## Monthly Deadlines (if applicable)

### Sales Tax
- **Due:** 20th of following month (if monthly filer)
- **Arkansas:** File and pay via Arkansas Taxpayer Access Point (ATAP)

### Bookkeeping
- **Best Practice:** Reconcile accounts by 5th of each month
- **Best Practice:** Review P&L and cash flow monthly

## Quarterly Tasks

- Update financial projections
- Review and categorize expenses
- Back up financial records

### Email Reminders

- Arkansas DFA

## Important Notes

**Estimated Taxes**
Required if you expect to owe $1,000+ in federal taxes. Calculate based on prior year's tax liability or current year's projected income.

**Franchise Tax**
Arkansas requires all LLCs to file annually, even if no business activity. Late filing incurs penalties.

**Record Retention**
Keep tax returns and supporting documents for at least 7 years.
