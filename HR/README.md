# Human Resources (HR)

This directory contains all HR policies, processes, and employee-related documentation.

## Purpose

The HR function is responsible for managing all people-related aspects of the company, including:
- Talent acquisition and recruiting
- Employee onboarding and offboarding
- Performance management
- Compensation and benefits
- Employee relations
- Company culture and engagement

## Key Documents

- **CLAUDE.md** - Detailed workflows and processes for HR operations
- **policies/** - Employee handbook and HR policies
- **recruiting/** - Job descriptions, interview guides, candidate pipeline
- **onboarding/** - Onboarding checklists and materials
- **performance/** - Performance review templates and processes
- **compensation/** - Salary bands, equity guidelines, bonus structure
- **benefits/** - Benefits information and enrollment
