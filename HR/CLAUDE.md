# Human Resources Function - Claude Workflows

This directory contains all HR-related policies, processes, and workflows.

## Primary Responsibilities

- Talent acquisition and recruiting
- Onboarding and offboarding
- Employee relations and engagement
- Performance management
- Compensation and benefits administration
- Learning and development
- HR compliance and policies
- Culture and values
- Diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI)
- Organizational development

## Key Workflows

### Hiring Process
1. Define role requirements and job description
2. Post on job boards and source candidates
3. Screen resumes and conduct phone screens
4. Coordinate technical/functional interviews
5. Check references
6. Prepare offer letter and negotiate
7. Complete background check
8. Obtain signed offer letter
9. Prepare for onboarding

### Employee Onboarding
1. Send welcome email with start details
2. Prepare workspace and equipment
3. Set up accounts and system access
4. Schedule orientation and training
5. Assign onboarding buddy
6. Review handbook and policies
7. Complete paperwork (I-9, W-4, benefits)
8. Introduce to team
9. Set 30/60/90 day goals
10. Check in regularly during first months

### Performance Review Cycle
1. Set review schedule (typically bi-annual)
2. Distribute self-assessment forms
3. Collect peer feedback (360 if applicable)
4. Managers complete evaluations
5. Calibrate ratings across teams
6. Conduct 1-on-1 review meetings
7. Document outcomes and development plans
8. Process compensation changes
9. Track progress on development goals

### Offboarding Process
1. Receive resignation or initiate separation
2. Conduct exit interview
3. Create offboarding checklist
4. Coordinate final day logistics
5. Collect equipment and access badges
6. Revoke system access
7. Process final paycheck and benefits
8. Ensure knowledge transfer
9. Update organizational chart
10. Archive employee file

### Benefits Administration
1. Enroll new employees in benefits
2. Process qualifying life events
3. Conduct annual benefits enrollment
4. Manage benefits vendor relationships
5. Communicate benefits information
6. Resolve benefits issues
7. Track benefits costs
8. Review and renew plans annually

## Recruiting Best Practices

### Sourcing
- Use multiple channels (job boards, LinkedIn, referrals)
- Build talent pipeline proactively
- Leverage employee referrals (highest quality)
- Attend relevant conferences and events
- Build employer brand

### Interviewing
- Use structured interview guides
- Include diverse interview panel
- Assess for skills, values fit, and potential
- Take detailed notes
- Compare candidates objectively
- Check references thoroughly

### Offer Process
- Move quickly for top candidates
- Be transparent about compensation
- Highlight growth opportunities
- Address concerns proactively
- Create excitement about role and company

## Onboarding Best Practices

1. **Pre-boarding** - Engage before day one
2. **First day** - Make it special and welcoming
3. **First week** - Focus on orientation and setup
4. **First month** - Training and relationship building
5. **90 days** - Check progress against goals
6. **Continuous** - Support through first year

## Performance Management

### Goal Setting (OKRs recommended)
- Set ambitious but achievable goals
- Align individual, team, and company goals
- Make goals specific and measurable
- Review and update quarterly
- Track progress transparently

### Feedback Culture
- Encourage regular 1-on-1s (weekly or bi-weekly)
- Provide real-time feedback
- Balance positive and constructive
- Focus on behaviors and impact
- Document significant conversations

### Development Planning
- Identify strengths and growth areas
- Create actionable development plans
- Provide learning opportunities
- Support career progression
- Invest in training and coaching

## Integration Points

- **Finance**: Payroll, benefits costs, headcount planning
- **Legal**: Employment contracts, compliance, policies
- **Admin**: Workspace, equipment, vendor management
- **All Teams**: Hiring needs, performance feedback, culture

## Key Metrics to Track

- Headcount and growth
- Time to hire
- Cost per hire
- Offer acceptance rate
- Employee retention rate
- Voluntary vs. involuntary turnover
- Employee satisfaction/engagement scores
- Diversity metrics
- Training hours per employee
- Performance rating distribution

## Compensation Philosophy

### Components
1. **Base Salary** - Competitive with market
2. **Equity** - Ownership in company success
3. **Bonus** - Performance-based rewards
4. **Benefits** - Health, wellness, retirement
5. **Perks** - Additional employee benefits

### Principles
- Pay fairly and competitively
- Use consistent salary bands
- Consider geography (remote vs. office)
- Reward high performers
- Be transparent about philosophy
- Review compensation regularly

## Company Culture

### Values Definition
- Define 3-5 core company values
- Make them specific and actionable
- Integrate into all HR processes
- Recognize values-aligned behavior
- Hold team accountable

### Building Culture
- Lead by example from top
- Hire for values fit
- Celebrate wins together
- Create team bonding opportunities
- Encourage open communication
- Support work-life balance
- Invest in employee development
- Recognize and reward contributions

## DEI Best Practices

1. **Recruiting** - Diverse candidate pipelines and interview panels
2. **Hiring** - Structured interviews to reduce bias
3. **Development** - Equal access to growth opportunities
4. **Promotion** - Transparent criteria and processes
5. **Pay equity** - Regular audits and adjustments
6. **Inclusion** - Create belonging for all employees
7. **Education** - DEI training and awareness
8. **Measurement** - Track metrics and progress

## Red Flags to Avoid

- Inconsistent policy application
- Poor documentation
- Delayed or incomplete onboarding
- Lack of regular feedback
- Unclear expectations
- Compliance violations
- High turnover without analysis
- Toxic culture indicators
- Pay inequities
- Legal liability risks
