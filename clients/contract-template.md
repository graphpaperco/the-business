## 1. SERVICES

1.1 **Scope of Services.** Consultant agrees to provide the services ("Services") described in one or more Statements of Work ("SOW") attached to and incorporated into this Agreement. Each SOW shall specify:
   - Description of services
   - Deliverables
   - Timeline and milestones
   - Fees and payment terms
   - Any additional terms specific to that engagement

1.2 **Standard of Performance.** Consultant shall perform Services in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards.

---

## 2. COMPENSATION & PAYMENT

2.1 **Fees.** Client agrees to pay Consultant the fees specified in each SOW.

2.2 **Payment Terms.** Unless otherwise specified in an SOW:
   - Invoices shall be sent [bi-weekly/monthly/per milestone]
   - Payment is due within fifteen (15) days of invoice date ("Net 15")
   - Late payments subject to 1.5% monthly interest charge

2.3 **Expenses.** Client shall reimburse pre-approved expenses at cost plus [10%] markup. Consultant shall provide receipts for expenses over $[amount].

2.4 **Taxes.** Fees do not include sales tax, VAT, or similar taxes. Client is responsible for all applicable taxes except Consultant's income taxes.

---

## 3. TERM & TERMINATION

3.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated by either party.

3.2 **Termination for Convenience.** Either party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW with thirty (30) days' written notice.

3.3 **Termination for Cause.** Either party may terminate immediately upon written notice if the other party:
   - Materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within ten (10) days of written notice
   - Becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy

3.4 **Effect of Termination.** Upon termination:
   - Client shall pay for all Services performed through termination date
   - Consultant shall deliver all work product completed as of termination date
   - Sections 4 (Intellectual Property), 5 (Confidentiality), 7 (Warranties), and 8 (Limitation of Liability) shall survive

---

## 4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

4.1 **Ownership of Deliverables.** Upon full payment of all fees:
   - Client owns all work product and deliverables created specifically for Client under this Agreement
   - Consultant assigns all right, title, and interest in such deliverables to Client

4.2 **Consultant's Pre-Existing IP.** Consultant retains ownership of:
   - Pre-existing tools, frameworks, code, and methodologies
   - General knowledge and skills gained during performance

4.3 **License to Pre-Existing IP.** Consultant grants Client a perpetual, non-exclusive license to use any Consultant pre-existing IP incorporated into deliverables.

4.4 **Open Source.** Any open source components remain subject to their original licenses. Consultant shall identify all open source components used in deliverables.

4.5 **Portfolio Rights.** Consultant may use Client's name and project description as a portfolio piece and case study with Client's prior written approval.

---

## 5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 **Definition.** "Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one party to the other, including but not limited to business plans, financial information, customer data, technical information, and trade secrets.

5.2 **Obligations.** Each party agrees to:
   - Maintain confidentiality of the other party's Confidential Information
   - Use Confidential Information solely for purposes of this Agreement
   - Protect Confidential Information with same care as own confidential information (but no less than reasonable care)

5.3 **Exceptions.** Confidentiality obligations don't apply to information that:
   - Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of receiving party
   - Is rightfully received from a third party without confidentiality restrictions
   - Is independently developed without use of Confidential Information
   - Is required to be disclosed by law or court order (with notice to disclosing party)

5.4 **Term.** Confidentiality obligations continue for three (3) years after termination of this Agreement.

---

## 6. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR

6.1 **Relationship.** Consultant is an independent contractor, not an employee, agent, or partner of Client.

6.2 **No Benefits.** Consultant is not entitled to employee benefits, unemployment insurance, workers' compensation, or tax withholding.

6.3 **Taxes.** Consultant is responsible for all taxes, licenses, and permits related to Services.

6.4 **Control.** Consultant has sole discretion over manner and means of performing Services, subject to delivering agreed-upon results.

---

## 7. WARRANTIES & DISCLAIMERS

7.1 **Mutual Warranties.** Each party warrants that:
   - It has full authority to enter into this Agreement
   - Its performance will not violate any agreement with third parties

7.2 **Consultant Warranties.** Consultant warrants that:
   - Services will be performed in professional manner consistent with industry standards
   - Deliverables will be free from material defects for [30/60/90] days after delivery
   - Deliverables will not infringe third-party intellectual property rights

7.3 **Warranty Remedy.** For defective deliverables, Consultant's sole obligation is to re-perform Services or refund fees paid for defective portion.

7.4 **DISCLAIMER.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET FORTH ABOVE, CONSULTANT PROVIDES SERVICES "AS IS" WITHOUT WARRANTY OF ANY KIND, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR PARTICULAR PURPOSE, OR NON-INFRINGEMENT.

---

## 8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

8.1 **Cap on Liability.** CONSULTANT'S TOTAL LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID BY CLIENT IN THE SIX (6) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

8.2 **Exclusion of Damages.** IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOST PROFITS, EVEN IF ADVISED OF POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.

8.3 **Exceptions.** Limitations in this section don't apply to:
   - Either party's indemnification obligations
   - Claims arising from willful misconduct or gross negligence
   - Claims arising from breach of confidentiality

---

## 9. INDEMNIFICATION

9.1 **By Consultant.** Consultant shall indemnify Client against third-party claims that deliverables infringe third-party intellectual property rights, provided Client:
   - Promptly notifies Consultant of claim
   - Gives Consultant sole control of defense
   - Provides reasonable cooperation

9.2 **By Client.** Client shall indemnify Consultant against third-party claims arising from:
   - Client's use of deliverables outside scope of this Agreement
   - Client-provided content or materials
   - Modifications to deliverables not made by Consultant

---

## 10. GENERAL PROVISIONS

10.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of [Arkansas] without regard to conflict of law provisions.

10.2 **Dispute Resolution.** The parties shall attempt to resolve disputes through good faith negotiation. If unsuccessful within thirty (30) days, disputes may be resolved through [mediation/arbitration/litigation].

10.3 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement and attached SOWs constitute the entire agreement and supersede all prior agreements or understandings.

10.4 **Amendments.** Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.

10.5 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent, except to a successor in a merger or acquisition.

10.6 **Severability.** If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full effect.

10.7 **Waiver.** Failure to enforce any provision doesn't waive future enforcement.

10.8 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and sent to addresses above via email (with confirmation) or certified mail.

10.9 **Force Majeure.** Neither party is liable for delays due to circumstances beyond reasonable control (natural disasters, pandemics, government actions, etc.).

10.10 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including electronic signatures.
