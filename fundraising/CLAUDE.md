# Fundraising Function - Claude Workflows

This directory contains all fundraising-related materials, processes, and workflows.

## Primary Responsibilities

- Fundraising strategy and planning
- Investor pipeline management
- Pitch deck development and maintenance
- Data room preparation and management
- Term sheet negotiation support
- Due diligence coordination
- Cap table management
- Fundraising documentation
- Investor relationship building (pre-investment)

## Key Workflows

### Fundraising Campaign Management
1. Define fundraising goals (amount, timing, terms)
2. Create target investor list (stage, sector, geography)
3. Develop compelling pitch deck and materials
4. Build investor pipeline in CRM
5. Schedule and track meetings
6. Follow up systematically
7. Move investors through funnel
8. Close the round

### Pitch Deck Development
1. Start with standard template
2. Craft compelling narrative (problem/solution/traction)
3. Include key metrics and projections
4. Design visually appealing slides
5. Tailor for different audiences
6. Update with latest traction
7. Version control all changes
8. Prepare multiple lengths (full, summary, teaser)

### Data Room Preparation
1. Create organized folder structure
2. Gather all required documents
3. Ensure everything is current
4. Remove sensitive/unnecessary information
5. Set up access controls
6. Test data room functionality
7. Prepare document index
8. Brief team on contents

### Investor Pipeline Management
1. Research and identify target investors
2. Get warm introductions when possible
3. Track all investor interactions
4. Categorize by interest level
5. Follow up consistently
6. Move hot leads forward quickly
7. Keep investors engaged
8. Update pipeline status regularly

### Due Diligence Support
1. Respond promptly to all requests
2. Organize answers clearly
3. Coordinate with legal, finance, product teams
4. Track outstanding items
5. Flag any concerns early
6. Maintain communication rhythm
7. Keep process moving forward

## Fundraising Stages

### Pre-Seed / Seed
- Focus: Product-market fit, founding team, early traction
- Deck emphasis: Problem, solution, why now, team
- Key metrics: User growth, engagement, early revenue
- Typical asks: $500K - $3M

### Series A
- Focus: Business model validation, scaling
- Deck emphasis: Traction, unit economics, growth plan
- Key metrics: Revenue growth, CAC/LTV, retention
- Typical asks: $3M - $15M

### Series B+
- Focus: Market leadership, efficient growth
- Deck emphasis: Market opportunity, competitive moats, path to profitability
- Key metrics: ARR growth, efficiency ratios, market share
- Typical asks: $15M+

## Best Practices

1. **Start early** - Fundraising takes 3-6 months
2. **Build relationships** - Engage investors before you need money
3. **Create urgency** - Run a tight process with deadline
4. **Be transparent** - Address concerns head-on
5. **Show momentum** - Regular updates on traction
6. **Multiple options** - Don't rely on single investor
7. **Know your numbers** - Master the financial model
8. **Practice pitch** - Rehearse until it's natural
9. **Quick follow-up** - Send materials within 24 hours
10. **Learn and iterate** - Improve based on feedback

## Investor Engagement Tips

- Get warm introductions (2-3x higher success rate)
- Research investor thesis and portfolio
- Lead with traction and metrics
- Tell memorable stories
- Be concise and respect their time
- Handle objections confidently
- Always have clear next steps
- Follow up consistently
- Keep investors updated on progress
- Build FOMO (fear of missing out)

## Red Flags to Avoid

- Messy or incomplete data room
- Outdated financial information
- Poor understanding of metrics
- No clear use of funds
- Unrealistic projections
- Slow response times
- Team alignment issues
- Legal or IP problems
