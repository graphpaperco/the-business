# Investor Relations Function - Claude Workflows

This directory contains all investor relations materials, communications, and workflows.

## Primary Responsibilities

- Regular investor updates and communications
- Board meeting preparation and materials
- Investor reporting and metrics tracking
- Shareholder communication
- Cap table maintenance
- Board governance and compliance
- Investor inquiries and requests
- Annual shareholder meetings
- Ongoing investor relationship management

## Key Workflows

### Monthly Investor Update
1. Collect key metrics and highlights from all teams
2. Draft update using standard template
3. Include: metrics, wins, challenges, asks
4. Review with leadership team
5. Send by consistent day each month
6. Archive in investor-updates folder
7. Track opens and responses
8. Follow up on investor replies

### Board Meeting Preparation
1. Schedule meeting 4-6 weeks in advance
2. Create agenda with board input
3. Collect materials from department heads
4. Prepare board deck (typically 2 weeks before)
5. Send materials 3-5 days before meeting
6. Brief presenters on timing and format
7. Conduct meeting professionally
8. Distribute minutes within 1 week

### Quarterly Business Review
1. Prepare comprehensive performance review
2. Analyze metrics vs. plan
3. Update financial projections
4. Review strategic initiatives
5. Identify risks and opportunities
6. Present to board and/or investors
7. Document decisions and action items
8. Follow up on commitments

### Investor Inquiry Response
1. Acknowledge inquiry promptly
2. Gather required information
3. Coordinate with relevant teams
4. Provide clear, complete answer
5. Follow up to ensure satisfaction
6. Document inquiry and response
7. Update materials if needed

### Cap Table Management
1. Maintain single source of truth
2. Update after each financing event
3. Track all option grants and exercises
4. Model dilution scenarios
5. Prepare for board review
6. Ensure legal documentation matches
7. Provide to investors on request

## Claude Assistant Instructions

When working in the investor-relations directory:

1. **Communicate regularly** - Consistent updates build trust
2. **Be transparent** - Share good news and bad
3. **Respect time** - Be concise and well-organized
4. **Follow through** - Track and complete commitments
5. **Maintain confidentiality** - Handle sensitive information carefully
6. **Professionalism** - High-quality materials and communications
7. **Proactive** - Anticipate questions and concerns
8. **Responsive** - Quick replies to investor inquiries

## Monthly Update Template

### Executive Summary
- One paragraph: current state, key wins, main challenges

### Key Metrics
- Revenue/ARR
- Customer metrics (new, churn, NPS)
- Product usage
- Team size
- Cash and runway

### Highlights (Top 3)
- Customer wins
- Product launches
- Team additions
- Press/recognition
- Fundraising progress

### Challenges (Top 2-3)
- Be honest about issues
- Explain mitigation plans
- Ask for help if needed

### Focus for Next Month
- Top 3 priorities
- Key milestones

### Asks
- Specific ways investors can help
- Introductions, advice, resources

## Board Meeting Best Practices

### Before Meeting
- Set clear agenda with time allocations
- Send materials 3-5 days in advance
- Allow time for board members to review
- Prepare presenters
- Anticipate questions

### During Meeting
- Start and end on time
- Follow agenda
- Focus on strategic topics
- Encourage board participation
- Document decisions clearly
- Assign action items

### After Meeting
- Distribute minutes within 1 week
- Follow up on action items
- File resolutions properly
- Update shareholders if needed

## Best Practices

1. **Consistency** - Same day each month for updates
2. **Transparency** - Share challenges, not just wins
3. **Data-driven** - Back up statements with metrics
4. **Forward-looking** - Include plans and projections
5. **Concise** - Respect investors' time
6. **Professional** - High-quality materials
7. **Responsive** - Reply to questions quickly
8. **Proactive** - Anticipate information needs
9. **Accessible** - Make it easy to find information
10. **Grateful** - Acknowledge investor support

## Investor Rights to Track

- Information rights (updates, financials)
- Board seats and observers
- Voting rights
- Pro-rata rights (future rounds)
- Inspection rights
- Registration rights (for exits)

## Red Flags to Avoid

- Inconsistent communication
- Surprising investors with bad news
- Missing board meetings or materials
- Ignoring investor inquiries
- Messy cap table
- Unprofessional materials
- Lack of transparency
- Over-promising and under-delivering
